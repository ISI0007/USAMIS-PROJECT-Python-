# Fictional profile illustrations

Ten original images were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 30 October 2026. The user selected illustrated avatars for fictional demo users. They are not photos of real students or teachers and are not used for biometric recognition or model training.

Location: `app/frontend/static/images/demo-avatars/`. Files `student-1.png` through `student-8.png` follow student-number order; `teacher-1.png` and `teacher-2.png` follow staff-number order. `scripts/complete_demo.py` assigns the SQL paths.

Common generation specification: one square illustrated profile avatar; warm professional expression; refined editorial illustration with subtle shading; centered head and shoulders; pale ivory background; generous margin; no text, logo or border; entirely fictional adult.

Individual variations: teal sweater and short dark hair; mustard cardigan and round glasses; blue sweater and short curls; coral blouse and shoulder-length dark hair; olive jacket and auburn hair; lavender top and dark braids; navy sweatshirt and glasses; cream cardigan and brown bob; male teacher with navy blazer, beard and glasses; female teacher with teal blazer and natural curls. The first eight represent adult fictional students, and the last two adult fictional teachers.

Original generated files remain in the Codex generated-images directory; the application includes independent local copies, so it does not depend on that directory.
